## Room Overview

A versatile large single room (suitable as a small double) located on the first-floor
half-landing. Featuring a peaceful north-facing aspect, this room is easily accessible
via the rear entrance with only a few exterior steps, though steps lead down to the
ground-floor dining room.

## Sleeping & Comfort

Bed: 1 Comfortable 4-Foot Double Bed  
Atmosphere: Non-smoking room with full central heating  
Housekeeping: Daily housekeeping with fresh linen and towels supplied

Bathroom: Private en-suite with shower, washbasin, toilet, in-room hairdryer, and complimentary toiletries  
Refreshments: Electric kettle, tea and coffee maker, and complimentary bottled water  
Entertainment & Connectivity: Flat-screen TV with cable channels, plus free high-speed broadband Wi-Fi
