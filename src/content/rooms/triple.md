Relax in our spacious 102 ft² room, perfectly sized for families or groups of up to three guests.
After a day exploring Torquay, you can unwind in the comfortable seating area, enjoy beautiful views
of the garden, and rest easily thanks to soundproof walls.

## Exceptional Comfort

Guests highly recommend our beds, giving them an 8.8/10 comfort rating (based on 89 reviews).
The room includes:

- 1 Large Double Bed (Extra-long, over 2 meters)
- 1 Single Bed (Extra-long, over 2 meters)

## Key Amenities

Bathroom: Private en-suite with a walk-in shower, complimentary premium toiletries, and a hairdryer.

Refreshments: Tea and coffee maker (with an electric kettle).

Comfort & Tech: Flat-screen TV, climate control (heating and fan), carpeted floors, and convenient bedside charging sockets.

Convenience: Wardrobe for storage, dedicated workspace/desk, and a wake-up service. (Iron and ironing board available on request).

## Dining Options

**Breakfast:** Fuel up for the day with a delicious, carefully prepared breakfast. It is available as an optional extra for just £10 per person, per day.
