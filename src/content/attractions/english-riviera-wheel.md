Towering gracefully above Torquay’s palm-lined seafront in **Princess Gardens**, the **[English Riviera Wheel](https://englishrivierawheel.co.uk/)** is one of Torbay’s most striking seasonal landmarks.

Standing nearly 150 feet high alongside the historic Princess Theatre and Torquay Marina, this giant observation wheel lifts visitors high into the sky for spectacular **360-degree panoramic vistas** stretching across the shimmering waters of Tor Bay, Torre Abbey Sands, Paignton, and all the way to Berry Head in Brixham.

---

## The Experience

- **Breathtaking Bird’s-Eye Views:** Enjoy an unhurried, multi-revolution flight offering unbeatable vantage points over Torquay Harbour, luxury yachts, and the dramatic South Devon coastline.
- **Comfortable, All-Weather Gondolas:** The wheel features modern, fully enclosed, weather-shielded gondolas that provide smooth, quiet rotation and crystal-clear photo opportunities in total comfort.
- **Accessible & Family-Friendly:** Suitable for all ages, with specially designed gondolas capable of accommodating wheelchairs and prams.
- **Magical by Day or Night:** Experience it under clear blue skies for panoramic sea views, or take a twilight spin as sunset colors wash across the bay and the wheel illuminates the waterfront.

---

## Seasonal Opening: Closed in Winter

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Seasonal Operation:** The English Riviera Wheel operates annually through the spring, summer, and early autumn months. It is **closed during the winter season** for scheduled maintenance and coastal winter storage, returning to Princess Gardens ahead of the spring season.
