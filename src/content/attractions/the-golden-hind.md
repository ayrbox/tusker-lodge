Proudly moored in the scenic waters of Brixham Harbour, the **[Golden Hind Museum Ship](https://www.goldenhind.co.uk/)** is a full-sized, five-deck replica of one of the most celebrated vessels in maritime history. 

Between 1577 and 1580, Devon-born privateer and explorer **[Sir Francis Drake](https://www.goldenhind.co.uk/sir-francis-drake.html)** commanded the *Golden Hind* (originally christened the *Pelican*) on an epic, perilous journey around the globe. He became the first Englishman to circumnavigate the earth, raiding Spanish galleons and claiming new lands before returning laden with treasure to be knighted aboard by Queen Elizabeth I. Today, this floating museum offers an immersive journey back to the high seas of the 16th century.

---

## Where It Is Located

The Golden Hind is berthed at **The Quayside, Brixham Harbour, Devon (TQ5 8AW)**, floating right in the heart of this historic South Devon fishing port. 

Because it is moored in tidal harbour waters, the ship actually rises and falls up to two metres with the daily ebb and flow of the English Channel tides, making the experience feel wonderfully authentic from the moment you step across the gangway.

---

## What to Explore Onboard

Stepping aboard the galleon strikes the perfect balance between living Tudor history and exciting hands-on fun for all ages:

- **Explore Five Authentic Decks:** Wander above and below decks to see where Drake and his 70-man crew lived, worked, and survived for nearly three years at sea.
- **Captain's Cabin & Quarters:** Inspect Drake’s private quarters, navigation instruments, maps, and weapons of the Elizabethan era.
- **The Gun Deck & Cargo Hold:** Peer into the cramped crew bunks, galley, weapons lockers, and cannon ports that helped Drake battle through Spanish waters.
- **Interactive Audio Tour:** Bring your smartphone along to access an engaging digital audio guide packed with seafaring stories and soundscapes as you explore.
- **Fun for Young Buccaneers:** Children love hunting for hidden skeletons across the decks on the *Skeleton Hunt*, collecting stamps for *Sid's Challenge*, and daring each other to "Walk the Plank"!

## Opening Times & Planning Your Visit

- **Seasonal Opening:** The ship operates from **February half term through the end of October**, with regular daily opening hours. It is **closed during the winter months** while essential timber maintenance, rigging restoration, and refitting take place.
- **Tickets:** Pre-booking is not required; tickets can be purchased directly at the harbour quayside kiosk on the day (cards and cash accepted). Discounted family tickets and senior concessions are available.
- **Dog-Friendly:** Well-behaved dogs on leads are welcome to join their owners on board.

## Visiting from Tusker Lodge

Located at **533 Babbacombe Road, Torquay (TQ1 1HQ)**, **[Tusker Lodge](/)** is a fantastic base for exploring the maritime charms of Brixham (approx. **8.5 miles** around Tor Bay):

- **By Car (20–25 minutes):** Follow the coastal A379 / A3022 around Tor Bay directly into Brixham. Park at **Brixham Central Car Park** (short stay) or **Freshwater Quarry / Oxen Cove** for an easy stroll past the harbour.
- **By Passenger Ferry (Seasonal):** Hop on the **Western Lady Ferry** from Torquay Harbour directly across the bay to Brixham Quayside—landing just steps from the Golden Hind!
- **By Bus (25–30 minutes):** Catch the frequent **Stagecoach Route 12** ("The Hop") from Torquay Harbour to Brixham town centre.

Combine your visit aboard the Golden Hind with fresh fish and chips on the quay, a walk along the breakwater, and a visit to the nearby **[Brixham Heritage Museum](/attractions/brixham-museum)** for a quintessential South Devon day out.
