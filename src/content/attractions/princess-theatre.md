Commanding a prime spot on Torbay Road right beside Princess Gardens and Torquay Marina (**TQ2 5EZ**), **[Princess Theatre](https://www.atgtickets.com/venues/princess-theatre-torquay/)** is South Devon's largest theatre and the English Riviera's premier venue for major touring productions.

---

## A Brief History of Princess Theatre

Before the sleek, glass-fronted building stood on the seafront, holidaymakers flocked to a giant summer marquee in Princess Gardens for light entertainment. Built by Torbay Council as the centrepiece of the seafront redevelopment, Princess Theatre officially opened on **7 June 1961** with a star-studded bill topped by **Morecambe & Wise** and **Tommy Cooper**.

Just two years later, on **18 August 1963**, **The Beatles** played two unforgettable concerts here at the height of Beatlemania—followed over the decades by legends from Cilla Black and Ken Dodd to modern West End hits. Following a £500,000 auditorium refurbishment in 2024, the 1,500-seat theatre continues to shine as a flagship regional stage.

## Shows & Activities to Expect

- **West End Musicals & Touring Plays:** Major national tours direct from London's West End, including hit musicals, acclaimed dramas, and thriller productions.
- **Stand-Up Comedy & Live Concerts:** Household-name comedians, touring pop and rock icons, dance spectaculars, and tribute shows.
- **Traditional Christmas Pantomime:** Big-budget festive pantomimes packed with spectacle and family fun.
