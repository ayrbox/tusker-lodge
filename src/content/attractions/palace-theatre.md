Situated on Palace Avenue in the heart of Paignton (**TQ3 3HF**), **[Palace Theatre](https://www.palacetheatrepaignton.co.uk/)** proudly holds the title of **Torbay's oldest working theatre**. With a cosy 380-seat proscenium-arch auditorium, it offers a lively, year-round calendar of live entertainment for all ages.

---

## A Brief History of Palace Theatre

Originally opened in **1890** as an 800-seat public hall (designed by local architect George S. Bridgman), the building has been at the centre of Paignton's social life for over 130 years. After a brief flirtation with becoming a film studio in 1919, it was purchased by Paignton Council in **1920**.

During World War II it was temporarily renamed **The Garrison Theatre**, and it was in that era that the Paignton Pantomime Company began staging festive shows there—a much-loved local tradition that continues every winter. Refurbished in 2005 and now run as a thriving community-focused venue, the Palace Theatre hosts around 100 performances a year.

## Shows & Activities to Expect

- **Touring Plays & Classic Drama:** Murder mysteries, Shakespearean productions, and local dramatic showcases.
- **Live Music, Comedy & Speaker Nights:** Tribute bands, acoustic concerts, stand-up comedy, and entertaining celebrity "in conversation" evenings.
- **Family Shows & Pantomimes:** Traditional Christmas pantomimes, children's workshops, ballet, and family matinees.
