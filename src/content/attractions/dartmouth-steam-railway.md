Few travel experiences evoke the golden age of British travel quite like the sound of a whistle echoing across Torbay as a vintage steam locomotive chugs into motion. The **[Dartmouth Steam Railway and River Boat Company](https://www.dartmouthrailriver.co.uk/)** is widely celebrated as one of the finest heritage railway and riverboat journeys in the UK, offering an unforgettable way to explore the dramatic coastline and river estuaries of the English Riviera.

Operating heritage steam locomotives, vintage passenger carriages, scenic river cruisers, and the country's last operational coal-fired paddle steamer, this multi-award-winning attraction links the seaside resort of **Paignton** with the historic maritime haven of **Dartmouth** and the bohemian market town of **Totnes**.

---

## The Steam Train Journey: Paignton to Kingswear

The railway line spans **seven miles** of breathtaking South Devon scenery:

- **Coastal Panoramas:** Departing from Paignton (Queen's Park Station), the train skirts the sandy beaches of Goodrington and Broadsands, chugging along the edge of the UNESCO English Riviera Global Geopark with sweeping views across Tor Bay.
- **Dart Estuary & Ancient Woodlands:** Crossing historic viaducts near Churston, the line turns inland, winding through lush woodland canopies that slope gently down to the peaceful waters of the River Dart.
- **The Ferry Crossing to Dartmouth:** The steam journey concludes at Kingswear Station, nestled on the water's edge. From here, passengers simply step aboard the passenger ferry (free of charge with your train ticket) for a delightful five-minute crossing across the river to **Dartmouth**.
- **Explore Maritime Dartmouth:** Once ashore, explore historic Bayard’s Cove, the 17th-century Butterwalk, royal naval heritage, independent art galleries, and waterfront bistros.

## The Famous "Round Robin" Day Trip

If you want the ultimate South Devon day out, book the legendary **[Round Robin Tour](https://www.dartmouthrailriver.co.uk/tickets/round-robin)**—consistently voted one of the top excursions in South West England.

This seamless circular journey lets you experience the region by rail, river, and road:
1. **Steam Train:** Paignton to Kingswear (approx. 30 mins).
2. **Passenger Ferry:** Kingswear across to Dartmouth (approx. 5 mins).
3. **River Dart Cruise:** Board a comfortable riverboat for a 90-minute scenic cruise along the tranquil waters between Dartmouth and Totnes, passing historic riverside estates, dense woodland, and spotting native seals and herons.
4. **Open-Top / Country Bus:** An open-top or country bus journey through picturesque Devon lanes connecting Totnes back to Paignton.

*(The Round Robin can be taken in either direction, and tickets allow plenty of time to explore Totnes and Dartmouth along the way!)*

## Historic Fleet Highlights

- **The UK's Only Coal-Fired Paddle Steamer:** Step back in time aboard the historic **[Paddle Steamer Kingswear Castle](https://www.dartmouthrailriver.co.uk/paddle-steamer)**. Built in 1924, this beautifully restored national treasure plies its original home waters of the River Dart on select seasonal dates.
- **Greenway Halt:** Fans of crime fiction can alight at the secluded woodland stop of Greenway Halt for a gentle walk to **Greenway House**, the beloved holiday home of **Agatha Christie**, now cared for by the National Trust.
- **The Devon Belle Observation Car:** Travel in luxury inside the 1917 vintage Pullman observation car featuring panoramic glass windows for unobstructed coastline views (available on select departures).
- **The Christmas Train of Lights:** During November and December, the railway transforms into the world-famous *Train of Lights*, where vintage carriages and locomotives are wrapped in thousands of synchronized, glowing LED lights for a dazzling evening ride.

## How to Visit from Tusker Lodge

Located at **533 Babbacombe Road, Torquay (TQ1 1HQ)**, **[Tusker Lodge](/)** is ideally situated for catching the steam train from Paignton (approx. **3.5 miles** away):

- **By Car (10–12 minutes):** Drive south via the Torquay seafront and A3022 straight into Paignton town centre. The main departure station is **Paignton (Queen's Park Station), Sat Nav: TQ4 6AF**. Plentiful multi-storey and pay-and-display parking is available adjacent to the station at Victoria Multi-Storey Car Park.
- **By Train (7 minutes):** Catch the local mainline train from Torquay Station to Paignton Station; the steam railway platform is immediately adjacent to the mainline station.
- **By Bus (15 minutes):** Catch the frequent **Stagecoach Route 12** ("The Hop") from Torquay Harbour directly to Paignton Bus Station, located right opposite the steam railway entrance.

Whether you are enjoying a romantic day out drifting down the Dart or treating the family to the thrill of heritage steam power, the Dartmouth Steam Railway is an absolute must-do during your stay at Tusker Lodge.
