Housed inside the striking Victorian Town Hall on New Road in Brixham (**TQ5 8TA**), **[Brixham Theatre](https://www.brixhamtheatre.org.uk/)** is an atmospheric 240-seat community theatre and cultural hub in the heart of South Devon's most famous fishing port.

---

## A Brief History of Brixham Theatre

Built in **1886** during Brixham's thriving Victorian fishing boom, the Italianate limestone building was designed by Torquay architect George S. Bridgman on the site of an old naval water reservoir. Over the decades, its lower levels served as a fish market and magistrate's court, while the spacious upper hall began life as a Victorian military drill hall before evolving into a beloved space for dances, public gatherings, and silent cinema.

Today, the Grade II listed building is home to Brixham Town Council and the volunteer-run **Brixham Theatre**, preserving more than a century of local heritage.

## Shows & Activities to Expect

- **Live Music & Tribute Bands:** Energetic evening concerts featuring popular UK tribute acts, folk singers, acoustic sets, and local musicians.
- **Stage Plays, Musicals & Panto:** Touring drama companies, one-person shows, and lively community theatre and pantomimes.
- **Community Cinema & Screenings:** Big-screen film nights alongside *National Theatre Live* and arts screenings.

## Planning Your Visit from Tusker Lodge

Located around **8.5 miles (20–25 minutes by car)** from **[Tusker Lodge](/)**—or a scenic ride on the **Stagecoach Route 12** bus or seasonal **Western Lady Ferry** from Torquay Harbour—Brixham Theatre is easy to weave into a coastal day out:

- **Combine History & Harbour Life:** Spend the afternoon exploring **[The Golden Hind](/attractions/the-golden-hind)** and **[Brixham Heritage Museum](/attractions/brixham-museum)**, enjoy fresh fish and chips on the quayside, and finish with an evening show at the theatre.
