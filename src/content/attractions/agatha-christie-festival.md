Every year, the English Riviera comes alive to honour its most famous daughter,
the "Queen of Crime," at the **International Agatha Christie Festival**. Hosted
across Torbay and South Devon, this vibrant annual celebration brings together
local residents and devoted mystery fans from across Britain and around the world
to celebrate Agatha Christie’s extraordinary life, legacy, and literary genius.

Organised by a registered charity—and proud partner of both the English Riviera
UNESCO Global Geopark and Exeter UNESCO City of Literature—the festival champions
engagement in literature, the arts, and the area’s rich cultural and geological
heritage. Visitors can immerse themselves in a packed programme featuring:

- **High-profile speakers** and fascinating literary talks
- **Live performances** of Christie’s classic plays and poetry
- **The Festival Fringe**, offering a wide variety of engaging arts, mystery, and literary experiences for all ages
- **Community projects** that support local creatives, schools, and Torbay’s vibrant heritage
