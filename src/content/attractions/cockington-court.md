Spanning over **450 acres of breathtaking country parkland** just a mile from Torquay seafront, **[Cockington Court](https://cockingtoncourt.org/)** is one of the English Riviera’s most cherished destinations. Combining a historic aristocratic manor, a vibrant hub of artisan craft makers, ornamental gardens, and a quintessential chocolate-box thatched village, it offers a wonderful, relaxed day out for couples, families, and dog owners alike.

Best of all, **admission to the grounds, Manor House, and craft studios is completely free!**

---

## Highlights & Things to Do

### 1. The Historic Manor House & Galleries
Steeped in centuries of history dating back to the Domesday Book, the 17th-century Manor House has been thoughtfully transformed into an open cultural and creative hub. Explore changing contemporary art exhibitions in the **Kitchen Gallery** and soak in the timeless architectural charm of the estate.

### 2. Meet the Creative Makers
Cockington Court is home to over twenty contemporary artist studios set across the Stable Yard and Cob Barn. Visitors can watch master artisans practicing their trades and purchase one-of-a-kind handmade pieces directly from the makers:
- **Artisan Crafts:** Discover glassblowers, stone carvers, ceramicists, sculptors, and bespoke leatherworkers.
- **Local Tastes:** Pop into *Chocolate Heaven Made in Devon* for handcrafted confectionery, or sample small-batch spirits at *Torbay Gin*.

### 3. Explore 450 Acres of Parkland & Estate Walks
Cared for in partnership with the Torbay Coast & Countryside Trust, the parkland features **five distinct walking trails** winding through:
- Picturesque ornamental lakes and historic bridges.
- Fragrant rose gardens and organic demonstration plots.
- Ancient woodland paths brimming with wild birds and native flora.
- The **Ark Play Area**, an inclusive playground tailored for children of all abilities.

### 4. Seven Dials Café
When it is time for a refreshment, relax at the **[Seven Dials Café](https://cockingtoncourt.org/seven-dials-cafe/)**:
- Treat yourself to a classic **Devonshire cream tea** served with warm, freshly baked homemade scones and local clotted cream.
- Enjoy barista coffees, specialty teas, light lunches, sandwiches, and local ales.
- Dine inside the cosy café or out on the sunny courtyard terrace overlooking the parkland. Special dog treats are available for four-legged guests!

### 5. Cockington Thatched Village
Just a short stroll from the Court gates lies **Cockington Village**, renowned as one of Britain’s prettiest villages. Wander past storybook thatched cottages, centuries-old tearooms, a traditional working blacksmith's forge, and the historic village cricket green.

## Dog-Friendly Days Out

Cockington Court has previously won the *Dog Friendly Business of the Year* award. Well-behaved dogs on leads are welcome across the extensive parkland trails, outdoor courtyards, and at the Seven Dials Café terrace.

- **Admission:** **FREE entry** to the country park, Manor House, and artisan craft studios.

- **Parking:** Dedicated pay-and-display car parks are available on site (Craft Centre, Cycle Hub, and Cockington Village car parks).
