Deep in the rugged heart of Dartmoor National Park lies one of Devon's most legendary natural wonders: **[Becky Falls Ancient Woodland Park](https://www.beckyfalls.com/)**. Welcoming explorers and nature lovers since 1903, this breathtaking 50-acre estate combines ancient temperate oak rainforest, a dramatic boulder-strewn river gorge, and a majestic 70-foot waterfall.

Whether you are looking for an exhilarating hike across granite boulders, a peaceful stroll under lush woodland canopies, or a fun-filled family wildlife encounter, Becky Falls makes an unforgettable nature-filled day trip from **[Tusker Lodge](/)**.

---

## The Main Attraction: Dartmoor's 70ft Waterfall

Carved out during the last Ice Age over 12,000 years ago, the waterfall sits at the very core of Becky Falls. Here, the rushing waters of **Becka Brook** tumble more than **70 feet (over 21 metres)** through a steep, dramatic ravine filled with colossal moss-covered granite boulders, before winding onward down into the scenic Bovey Valley.

- **Dramatic Cascades:** While the main 70ft waterfall is the showstopper, smaller cascades, bubbling pools, and rushing chutes flow right along the length of the brook.
- **Pure Dartmoor Magic:** Enclosed by prehistoric ferns and rare lichens, the falls are entirely natural and particularly thunderous and awe-inspiring after fresh rainfall.
- **Breathtaking Photography:** The interplay of dappled forest sunlight, cascading spray, and ancient granite creates an idyllic backdrop for photographers and romantic nature strolls.

## Explore the Colour-Coded Walking Trails

Becky Falls features **three circular, colour-coded walking trails** through its protected Site of Special Scientific Interest (SSSI). Each route caters to different fitness levels and appetites for adventure:

### 1. The Blue Trail (Gentle Woodland Stroll & Nature Challenge)
- **Time:** Approx. 30 minutes | **Pace:** Leisurely
- **The Experience:** This is the gentlest trail, taking you through ancient oak woodlands bursting with wildlife. In early spring, catch swathes of snowdrops, followed in April and May by a vivid, fragrant carpet of bluebells. Deer, woodpeckers, and woodland songbirds frequently make an appearance.
- **Children's Nature Trail Challenge:** Kids grab a challenge card at the start (near the main picnic area) and search for educational fact boards along the route. Complete the trail questions and activities to collect a **free prize** from the Gift Shop!

### 2. The Red Trail (The Main Waterfall Loop)
- **Time:** Approx. 45 minutes | **Pace:** Moderate
- **The Experience:** The signature route that leads you straight down 70 feet into the gorge to the foot of the iconic Becky Falls.
- **The Terrain:** Cross the wooden bridge over the babbling brook and ascend the opposing cliffside. The path uses natural granite boulders as stepping stones and threads around ancient tree roots—rugged, scenic, and immensely rewarding.

### 3. The Purple Trail (The Adventurer's Gorge & Lower Falls)
- **Time:** Approx. 60+ minutes | **Pace:** Energetic / Challenging
- **The Experience:** Branching off the Red Trail, the Purple route is custom-built for adventurous walkers. It descends further into the lower valley, unveiling intimate views of the lower cascades and river boulders.
- **The "Lung Buster":** Tackle the famous "lung-buster" climb back out of the ravine! With steeper ascents and uneven boulder paths, this trail guarantees an exhilarating workout amidst pristine wilderness.

> [!TIP]
> **Footwear Matters:** Because Becky Falls is a pristine, natural Site of Special Scientific Interest, paths are intentionally kept wild with tree roots, stone steps, and natural rock pathways. Sturdy walking shoes, trail runners, or hiking boots with good grip are strongly recommended.

## Wildlife & Animal Encounters

Beyond its waterfalls, Becky Falls is home to a dedicated **Animal Discovery Zone** featuring rescue animals and interactive encounters:

- **Meet the Resident Animals:** Say hello to inquisitive meerkats, adorable pygmy goats, giant tortoises, rabbits, guinea pigs, ferrets, and miniature ponies.
- **Reptiles & Exotics:** Step inside the indoor reptile house to see snakes, lizards, and creepy-crawlies up close.
- **Daily Keeper Talks & Feedings:** Throughout the open season, educational keeper talks give children and adults the chance to watch meerkats forage, learn about birds of prey, and participate in gentle animal handling sessions.

## On-Site Services & Facilities

### The Woodland Café
After working up an appetite on the trails, recharge at the on-site **Woodland Café**.
- Relax with views overlooking the woodlands and animal paddocks.
- Savor warm Devon pasties, freshly grilled paninis, hearty burgers, and artisan coffees.
- Indulge in traditional **Devonshire clotted cream teas** and homemade cakes.
- Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free dietary options are readily available.

### Picturesque Picnic Areas
Prefer to pack your own spread? Becky Falls welcomes picnickers:
- Dedicated wooden picnic benches are situated near the main entrance and woodland clearings.
- Ample grassy and shady spots are available throughout the grounds where you can unfurl a blanket next to the soothing sounds of running water.

### Children’s Birthday Parties
Looking for an unforgettable birthday destination for nature-loving children? Becky Falls hosts custom **Children's Birthday Party Packages**:
- Includes all-day admission to the park, trails, and animal exhibits.
- Access to a private themed party room or reserved outdoor picnic setting.
- Hands-on private animal encounters (such as meeting the meerkats or reptile handling).
- Optional party food packages, or you can bring your own birthday cake with refrigeration provided by the team.

### The Gift Shop
Browse a charming collection of Dartmoor crafts, locally made jams and honeys, pocket-money toys, wildlife guides, outdoor walking essentials, and nature souvenirs.

### Dog-Friendly Days Out
Devon is meant to be explored with man's best friend! Well-behaved dogs on leads are warmly welcomed across all 50 acres of outdoor trails, picnic grounds, and on the café terrace (only the indoor Animal Discovery Zone is restricted). Fresh water bowls are provided on site.

## Seasonal Opening & Winter Closure

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Closed in Winter:** Becky Falls closes its gates during the late autumn and winter months (typically from early November through to February half-term). During this downtime, the family-run team carries out essential woodland conservation, fallen-tree management, and habitat protection.
>
> The park re-opens every year in **February (in time for February Half Term)** and remains open daily from **10:00 AM to 5:00 PM** (last entry 4:00 PM) through to the end of October.

## The Perfect Day Trip from Tusker Lodge

Located in sunny Torquay on the English Riviera, **[Tusker Lodge](/)** offers the ideal seaside base from which to explore the wild majesty of Dartmoor. 

- **Distance:** Approximately **17 miles (27 km)** north-west of Tusker Lodge.
- **Driving Time:** An easy, picturesque **35 to 40-minute drive**.
- **Route:** From Tusker Lodge, take the A380 north toward Newton Abbot, join the A382 past Bovey Tracey, and follow the scenic winding roads up into the Dartmoor hills toward Manaton (**Sat Nav: TQ13 9UG**).
- **Parking:** Generous **free parking** is provided on site for cars, camper vans, and minibuses.

### A Romantic Escape for Couples
Couples seeking fresh air and dramatic scenery will love escaping the bustling coast for a few hours:
- Wander hand-in-hand along the secluded moss-draped riverbanks beneath ancient oak canopies.
- Stop for quiet moments beside the rushing falls and capture stunning couple photos against granite backdrops.
- Round off your woodland adventure with a traditional Devon cream tea at the Woodland Café or combine your trip with a visit to a historic thatched Dartmoor pub in nearby villages like **Manaton**, **Lustleigh**, or **Widecombe-in-the-Moor**.

### An Unforgettable Adventure for Families
If you are visiting Torquay with kids, Becky Falls delivers the ultimate screen-free day outdoors:
- Let young explorers scramble across ancient boulders, hunt for woodland clues, and earn their Nature Challenge prize badge.
- Get up close with meerkats, tortoises, and reptiles during interactive keeper talks.
- Spread out a family picnic in the shade while little ones watch the animals play.
- Free baby-carrier backpacks are available to loan from the gift shop for parents with infants (pushchairs are not suitable due to the natural terrain).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How far is Becky Falls from Tusker Lodge?
Becky Falls is approximately 17 miles from Tusker Lodge in Torquay, taking just 35 to 40 minutes by car via the A380 and A382 through Bovey Tracey.

### When is Becky Falls closed for winter?
Becky Falls closes for its winter conservation season from November until mid-February, reopening daily for the spring and summer season from February half term through the end of October (10:00 AM to 5:00 PM). Please check the [official website](https://www.beckyfalls.com/opening-times) to confirm specific reopening dates.

### Can I bring my dog?
Yes! Dogs on short leads are welcome throughout all outdoor woodland paths, waterfalls, and outdoor café seating. Dogs are only excluded from the indoor Animal Discovery Zone.

### Are the walking trails suitable for pushchairs or wheelchairs?
Because Becky Falls is a preserved, natural Site of Special Scientific Interest (SSSI), the woodland paths feature exposed tree roots, uneven granite rocks, and steep steps. They are **not** suitable for pushchairs, prams, or wheelchairs. However, complimentary child-carrier backpacks can be borrowed free of charge from the Gift Shop (refundable deposit required).

### Can we bring our own food?
Yes, visitors are welcome to bring their own picnic. Dedicated picnic tables and scenic woodland clearings are available, as well as the Woodland Café for hot food, Devon cream teas, and cold refreshments.

### How do I book tickets?
Tickets and season passes can be purchased online in advance or at the entrance gate. Visit **[www.beckyfalls.com](https://www.beckyfalls.com/)** for current prices and reservations.
