Torquay and the English Riviera hold an unmatched place in literary history as the birthplace and lifelong inspiration of **Dame Agatha Christie DBE** (1890–1976). Officially recognised by *Guinness World Records* as the best-selling fiction writer of all time—with more than two billion copies sold worldwide in over 100 languages—Christie continues to captivate readers across every generation. From the roaring twenties through her prolific bestsellers of the 1960s (such as *The Pale Horse*, *The Mirror Crack'd from Side to Side*, and *Hallowe'en Party*), her imagination was deeply rooted in the dramatic coastlines, grand villas, and tranquil waters of South Devon.

For readers, writers, and curious travellers alike, the English Riviera is a living open-air museum where Christie's most famous stories—and legendary sleuths Hercule Poirot and Miss Jane Marple—spring to life.

## Greenway: Agatha Christie’s Beloved Holiday Home

Nestled high on the wooded banks of the River Dart lies **[Greenway House & Garden](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/devon/greenway)**, preserved and lovingly maintained by the **National Trust**. Christie affectionately referred to Greenway as *"the loveliest place in the world,"* using it as the holiday retreat where she spent summers and Christmases with her family and second husband, archaeologist Sir Max Mallowan.

- **The House & Collections:** Step inside rooms preserved as if the family has just stepped out for a stroll. The house is brimming with their personal collections, from archaeological treasures unearthed in the Middle East to first-edition books, Tunbridgeware, fine silver, and ceramics.
- **The Romantic Woodland Gardens & Boathouse:** Wander through sweeping sub-tropical riverside gardens filled with camellias, rhododendrons, and rare trees down to the historic boathouse—the notorious murder setting featured in her novel *Dead Man's Folly* and filmed here for the iconic TV adaptation starring David Suchet.
- **Planning Your Visit:** Greenway operates a planned visit system to protect its tranquil setting. If arriving by car, **parking spaces must be pre-booked in advance** on the [National Trust Greenway website](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/devon/greenway). Alternatively, make the journey part of the adventure: travel from Torquay to Paignton, board the heritage [Dartmouth Steam Railway](https://www.dartmouthrailriver.co.uk/) straight to *Greenway Halt*, or arrive by river cruise from Dartmouth or Totnes.

## The Agatha Christie Mile: Coastal Walks for Couples & Families

Tracing the author’s real-life stomping grounds, the **[Agatha Christie Mile](https://www.southwestcoastpath.org.uk/walksdb/591/)** is an easy, picturesque 3.4-mile walking route along Torquay’s seaside promenade, developed in partnership with the **South West Coast Path**. 

As a young woman, Agatha spent hours swimming, roller-skating, and socialising along these shores—experiences that directly inspired bestsellers such as *The ABC Murders* and *The Body in the Library*.

### Why Couples & Families Love It
- **Gentle & Accessible:** Mostly flat, paved, and fully pushchair- and wheelchair-friendly, making it an effortless and delightful day out for families with young children as well as couples looking for a relaxed, romantic seaside stroll.
- **Historic Discovery Trail:** Follow bronze plaques embedded along the route connecting iconic landmarks, including:
  - **The Grand Hotel:** Where Agatha spent her wedding night with Archibald Christie on Christmas Eve in 1914.
  - **Princess Gardens & Pier:** Featuring Victorian promenade views, floral gardens, and the fountain mentioned in *The ABC Murders*.
  - **The Agatha Christie Bust:** A bronze sculpture situated near Cary Green overlooking the harbour.
  - **Beacon Cove:** Her favourite secluded cove for bathing and swimming.
  - **Torquay Museum:** Housing the UK's only dedicated Agatha Christie gallery, complete with Poirot's study props, handwritten letters, and rare first editions.
  - **Torre Abbey:** Home to the historic monastery gardens and the famous *Potent Plants Garden*, cultivating the poisonous botanical species featured throughout her intricate plots.
- **Seaside Fun Along the Way:** Along the walk, families can pause for local Devon dairy ice cream, relax on sandy beaches, take a spin on the English Riviera Wheel, or stop at harbourfront cafes with panoramic sea views.

## The International Agatha Christie Festival

Every September, Torbay hosts the **[International Agatha Christie Festival](https://www.iacf-uk.org/about-us)** (IACF) to celebrate her birthday (15 September). A registered charity partnering with both the English Riviera UNESCO Global Geopark and Exeter UNESCO City of Literature, the festival brings mystery lovers, writers, and cultural historians together from across the globe.

The festival programme offers an eclectic mix of:
- Fascinating talks by renowned contemporary crime authors and historians
- Classic theatrical plays, poetry readings, and open-air mystery screenings
- Creative writing masterclasses, murder mystery dinners, and family-friendly Fringe arts events
- Behind-the-scenes tours of historic venues linked to Christie’s life

## The English Riviera: A Haven for Readers and Writers

There is a unique magic to South Devon's coastline. With its sub-tropical palm trees, mild microclimate, turquoise bays, and dramatic cliff walks, it has long been a sanctuary for creative minds seeking calm, clarity, and narrative inspiration. Whether you want to curl up with a classic whodunnit on a quiet terrace overlooking the bay, draft your own novel inspired by coastal sea air, or retrace the footsteps of the world's most successful author, the English Riviera offers the perfect creative escape.
