Overlooking the dramatic coastline of Babbacombe Downs in Torquay (**TQ1 3LU**), **[Babbacombe Theatre](https://babbacombe-theatre.com/)** is one of the English Riviera's best-loved entertainment landmarks. Famous across the UK for keeping classic British seaside variety alive, this intimate 600-seat venue blends nostalgia, live music, and laugh-out-loud comedy just minutes from Tusker Lodge.

---

## A Brief History of Babbacombe Theatre

Before the theatre existed, holidaymakers gathered on Babbacombe Downs around a humble wooden bandstand built in 1920. As Torquay's popularity as a seaside resort boomed, Torquay Town Council constructed the permanent **Babbacombe Downs Concert Hall** in 1938–1939—keeping the roofline low so nearby residents wouldn't lose their sweeping views of Babbacombe Bay (which is why all auditorium seating remains on a single level today).

Opening on **27 May 1939**, the venue served as an RAF lecture hall during World War II before returning to seaside entertainment and launching early performances by household names such as **Bruce Forsyth** and **Jimmy Tarbuck**. Saved from closure in 1986, it now boasts one of the longest-running seasonal variety show formats in Britain.

## Shows & Activities to Expect

- **Classic Seaside Variety Shows:** High-energy productions combining live vocals, dance, comedy sketches, and dazzling costumes—perfect for a feel-good evening out.
- **Stand-Up Comedy & Tribute Concerts:** Year-round appearances from touring comedians, vocal harmony groups, and tribute acts celebrating iconic rock, pop, and soul legends.
- **Family & Festive Pantomimes:** Seasonal shows and traditional Christmas pantomimes ideal for all generations.

## Planning Your Evening from Tusker Lodge

Staying at **[Tusker Lodge](/)** (**533 Babbacombe Road, Torquay, TQ1 1HQ**) puts you only **1.4 miles (5 minutes by car or local bus straight up Babbacombe Road)** from Babbacombe Downs:

- **Make an Afternoon & Evening of It:** Arrive early to stroll along the clifftop promenade, ride the historic **[Babbacombe Cliff Railway](/attractions/babbacombe-cliff-railway)** down to Oddicombe Beach, or visit **[Babbacombe Model Village](/attractions/babbacombe-model-village)** before dinner at one of the nearby Downs pubs or bistros.
