Spanning **80 acres of stunning botanical grounds and natural valleys**, **[Paignton Zoo Environmental Park](https://www.paigntonzoo.uk/)** is one of the UK’s premier zoological gardens and a leading conservation charity operated by the Wild Planet Trust. Celebrating over a century of wildlife care since opening in 1923, the zoo is home to more than **1,800 incredible animals** representing hundreds of rare and endangered species from across the globe, surrounded by over 1,600 botanical plant varieties.

Whether you are planning a memorable day out with the children or a relaxing couple’s stroll through tranquil nature trails, Paignton Zoo offers an immersive full-day wildlife adventure just a short drive from **[Tusker Lodge](/)**.

---

## What to Expect: Four Wild Worlds

Paignton Zoo organizes its expansive 80-acre grounds into four immersive, colour-coded habitat zones, each replicating distinct ecosystems from around the globe:

### 1. Grasslands (The African & American Plains)
Journey through open landscapes and come eye-to-eye with the zoo’s largest giants:
- Gaze up at the magnificent herd of **giraffes** and marvel at the powerful **southern white rhinos**.
- Spot playful **Grant’s zebras**, **greater rheas**, and gentle **vicuñas** grazing across the expanding savannah paddocks.

### 2. Rainforests (The Canopy & Primates)
Step beneath lush canopies and winding paths to discover dramatic jungle wildlife:
- Meet majestic **Western lowland gorillas** and **Bornean orangutans** in expansive natural enclosures.
- Catch a glimpse of elusive **Sumatran tigers** and stroll through the interactive, open-air **Lemur Wood**.
- Detour onto the quiet **Clennon Gorge Nature Trail**, a serene slice of native ancient woodland teeming with local birdlife and native butterflies.

### 3. Wetlands & Crocodile Swamp
Explore species connected to rivers, lagoons, and marshlands:
- Encounter graceful **sitatungas** (aquatic antelopes), colourful waders, and charismatic **red pandas**.
- Brave the humid atmosphere of **Crocodile Swamp**, home to prehistoric reptiles, giant pythons, and tropical greenery.

### 4. Islands & Tropics
Explore fragile island ecosystems and learn how island evolution shaped rare species:
- Discover exotic primates, unique reptiles, amphibians, and curious nocturnal animals.
- In the Welcome grounds, admire the vivid pink plumage of the **Chilean flamingo** flock and watch lively gibbons swing across island perches.

## Why Families Love a Day at the Zoo

Paignton Zoo is tailor-made for an effortless, action-packed family day trip:
- **Jungle Fun Soft Play:** A spacious indoor soft-play arena with four separate zones specifically designed for infants, toddlers, explorers, and older adventurers (up to 10 years).
- **The Hangout Outdoor Play Area:** An inclusive, sustainably built outdoor playground featuring sensory climbing frames, swings, and slides accessible to children of all abilities, including SEN-friendly equipment.
- **Adventure Trails:** Playful balancing beams and mini-trails dotted along the walking routes keep little feet motivated between animal exhibits.
- **Daily Keeper Talks & Feeding Times:** Fascinating daily presentations where kids can watch their favourite animals eat and learn remarkable facts directly from the zookeepers.

## Why Adults & Couples Love Visiting

Beyond the animal exhibits, Paignton Zoo offers a peaceful and deeply rewarding experience for grown-ups:
- **World-Class Botanical Collections:** The zoo doubles as an accredited botanical park with thousands of exotic trees, subtropical shrubs, and tranquil garden walks.
- **Conservation with Purpose:** Every ticket directly funds the Wild Planet Trust’s international conservation breeding programmes and global habitat restoration projects.
- **Food & Refreshments:** Relax at the *Hangout Café* or the main lakeside restaurant offering locally sourced lunches, barista coffees, artisan sandwiches, and Devon ice creams.

## Visiting from Tusker Lodge

Located at **533 Babbacombe Road, Torquay (TQ1 1HQ)**, **[Tusker Lodge](/)** is around **5 miles** from the zoo gates:

- **By Car (12–15 minutes):** Follow the Torquay seafront along the A3022 into Paignton, continuing onto Totnes Road (**Sat Nav: TQ4 7EU**).
- **By Bus (20–25 minutes):** Catch the frequent **Stagecoach Route 12** from Torquay centre or harbour to Paignton Bus Station, followed by a quick connecting bus (Route 22 or Stagecoach Gold) directly to the zoo entrance.
- **By Train:** Take the 7-minute train ride from Torquay Station to Paignton Station, followed by a short 5-minute taxi or bus ride to the zoo.
