Just a short stroll from Tusker Lodge on Babbacombe Road, **[Torquay Museum](https://torquaymuseum.org/)** is Devon's oldest museum and one of the English Riviera's richest cultural treasures. Founded in 1844 by the Torquay Natural History Society and housed in an impressive Grade II listed Victorian building, it brings together world-famous literary history, Ice Age archaeology, and tales of daring global expeditions under one roof.

Whether you are a mystery fan, a history enthusiast, or looking for an engaging rainy-day activity near the harbour, Torquay Museum makes for an unforgettable visit right on our doorstep.

---

## Galleries & Highlights Inside

From ancient human fossils to iconic television props, the museum's permanent and rotating galleries offer something to captivate every age:

- **The Agatha Christie Gallery:** Torquay Museum is home to the **only permanent exhibition dedicated to Agatha Christie in the UK**. Celebrating the Torquay-born Queen of Crime, the gallery showcases over 700 items—including family archive photographs, rare first-edition books, handwritten letters, and screen-worn costumes from *Agatha Christie's Poirot* and *Miss Marple*. Highlights include David Suchet's silver swan walking stick and a recreation of Poirot's study and lounge set from the beloved ITV series.
- **The Time Ark & Kents Cavern Collection:** Explore the world's largest collection of artefacts unearthed at nearby **[Kents Cavern](/attractions/kents-cavern)**, including discoveries from pioneering geologist William Pengelly's 19th-century excavations. A centrepiece of the collection is the **Kents Cavern Jawbone (KC-4)**—dated to over 41,000 years old and recognised as the oldest known *Homo sapiens* fossil in Northwest Europe.
- **The Explorers Gallery & Ancient Egypt:** Journey around the globe through collections inspired by Victorian and Edwardian explorers, including Torquay-born adventurer Lt. Col. Percy Harrison Fawcett, alongside fascinating Ancient Egyptian artefacts featuring a child's sarcophagus.
- **The Old Devon Farmhouse:** Step back into 19th-century rural Devon in this immersive life-size reconstruction, complete with period furniture, hearth, and traditional domestic tools.
- **Temporary Exhibitions, Café & Gift Shop:** Throughout the year, the museum hosts rotating exhibitions covering art, local history, and pop culture, alongside family trails, **Pengelly's Café**, and a well-stocked gift shop featuring local books and Agatha Christie souvenirs.

## Tickets, Opening Times & Planning Your Visit

Torquay Museum offers great value for families and returning guests:

- **Admission ("Pay Once, Get in Free for a Year"):** Standard tickets include **12 months of unlimited return visits** to all galleries and exhibitions (with free entry for under-5s and concessions available). See the latest ticket prices and book online on the **[Torquay Museum website](https://torquaymuseum.org/)**.
- **Opening Times:** Typically open **Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 AM – 4:45 PM** (last entry at 4:00 PM), and closed on Mondays.
- **Location:** 529 Babbacombe Road, Torquay, Devon, TQ1 1HG.
- **Parking:** There is no public car park on-site (one forecourt space can be pre-booked for visitors with disabilities). If driving, the nearest municipal car park is **Harbour / Terrace Car Park** on Montpelier Road (TQ1 1DD), a short walk away.

## Visiting from Tusker Lodge

Located just off Babbacombe Road on St Lukes Road, **[Tusker Lodge](/)** is practically neighbours with Torquay Museum:

- **On Foot (5–10 minutes):** Leave the car behind—it is an easy, pleasant walk of around half a mile from our front door straight to the museum entrance.
- **Make a Day of It:** Pair your museum visit with a stroll down to Torquay Harbour, follow the **[Agatha Christie Mile](/attractions/agatha-christie)** along the seafront, or head inland to explore **[Kents Cavern](/attractions/kents-cavern)** in person!
