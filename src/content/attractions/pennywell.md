Nestled in the rolling South Devon countryside just outside Buckfastleigh, **[Pennywell Farm](https://pennywellfarm.co.uk/)** is widely celebrated as Devon’s premier award-winning family attraction. Famed across the globe for its adorable miniature pigs and hands-on animal experiences, this lively 50-acre farm offers a joyful, interactive day out for visitors of all ages.

With **a new activity starting every 30 minutes** and all rides included in your ticket price, it is one of the most action-packed day trips in the region.

---

## What to Expect at Pennywell Farm

### 1. Home of the World-Famous Pennywell Miniature Pigs
- **World-Renowned "Piggy Cuddles":** Grab a warm blanket, settle into the quiet cuddle barn, and enjoy precious one-on-one time snuggling an adorable miniature piglet. It is an unforgettable experience beloved equally by adults, couples, and children.
- **Cheer on Miniature Pig Racing:** Gather around the show field every afternoon to watch the miniature pigs race full pelt over hurdles to reach their food bowls—accompanied by hilarious commentary and cheering crowds!

### 2. A New Activity Every 30 Minutes
From morning to late afternoon, a half-hourly bell announces the next hands-on activity (all included with admission):
- Bottle-feed hungry goat kids and lambs.
- Take friendly pygmy goats for a walk on a lead.
- Pamper the miniature ponies, pet heavy horses, and meet the Highland cattle.
- Hand-feed the deer herd in the deer park, try pond dipping, and cheer on high-speed ferret racing.

### 3. Four Free Farm Rides (Included with Entry)
There are no hidden ride tokens or coin slots at Pennywell—all four rides are included:
- **The Tractor & Trailer Ride:** A scenic rumble through the surrounding Devon hills.
- **The Runaway Train / Rainbow Railway:** A cheerful family railway winding past the animal paddocks.
- **The Red Rocket Ride:** A thrilling spinning rocket ride for children and adventurous adults.
- **Tractor Go-Karts:** Pedal-powered karting fun for young racers.

### 4. Indoor & Outdoor Play Whatever the Weather
- **Play Barns & Willow Maze:** Explore extensive indoor soft-play barns, outdoor climbing towers, bouncy trampolines, and navigate the willow maze.
- **Cafés & Picnics:** Refuel at *Noah’s Pantry* or *The Hen’s Nest* with local Devon pasties, homemade cakes, and barista coffees, or bring your own food to enjoy across spacious outdoor picnic lawns.

## Events & Seasonal Openings

Pennywell Farm opens daily from **mid-February through to late October/early November**, welcoming families and couples throughout the spring, summer, and autumn seasons.

- **Spring & Summer:** Packed with newborn baby animals, chick handling, and outdoor farm fun.
- **Magical Christmas Experience (Nov–Dec):** Towards the end of the year, the farm is transformed into a twinkling winter wonderland featuring an enchanting Christmas nativity journey, festive treats, and a visit to Father Christmas.

## Visiting from Tusker Lodge

Located at **533 Babbacombe Road, Torquay (TQ1 1HQ)**, **[Tusker Lodge](/)** is a convenient base for a day trip to Pennywell Farm:

- **Distance:** Approximately **14 miles (23 km)** west of Torquay.
- **Driving Time:** An easy **25- to 30-minute drive**.
- **Route:** From Tusker Lodge, take the A380 toward Newton Abbot, then the A385 through Totnes, joining the A38 toward Buckfastleigh (**Sat Nav: TQ11 0LT**).
- **Free Parking:** Free, generous on-site parking is available for all visitors.
