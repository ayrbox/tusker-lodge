Step into the deep past at **[Kents Cavern](https://www.kents-cavern.co.uk/caves)**—one of Europe's most significant prehistoric sites and proudly celebrated as **Britain’s Oldest Home**. 

Hidden beneath the rolling hills of Torquay, this world-renowned subterranean labyrinth was carved out over 2.5 million years ago within 400-million-year-old Devonian limestone. For hundreds of thousands of years, these chambers offered shelter to early humans and Ice Age beasts from harsh glacial elements, leaving behind ancient secrets, prehistoric tools, and rare fossils.

Because of its unparalleled historical and geological importance, Kents Cavern is legally protected as a **Site of Special Scientific Interest (SSSI)**, a **Scheduled Ancient Monument**, and serves as a flagship gateway for the **UNESCO English Riviera Global Geopark**.

---

## Ancient History & Subterranean Secrets

Guided tours take you deep into the atmospheric cavern network, where a natural, comfortable temperature of **14°C (57°F)** is maintained year-round:

- **Britain’s Earliest Human Dwellers:** Discover evidence of three different human species spanning over 500,000 years of occupation, from *Homo heidelbergensis* to Neanderthals and early *Homo sapiens*.
- **The Famous 41,000-Year-Old Jawbone:** Kents Cavern yielded the oldest anatomically modern human fossil discovered in Northwestern Europe—a prehistoric human maxilla (jawbone) dating back over 41,000 years.
- **Ice Age Beasts:** Gaze upon fossil remains and teeth from ferocious creatures that once prowled Torquay, including sabre-toothed cats, woolly mammoths, cave bears, and woolly rhinos.
- **Spectacular Geology:** Marvel at soaring stalagmites, delicate stalactites, the Long Arcade, and the Cave of Inscriptions, sealed over millennia by calcite floors.

## The Woodland Trail

Above ground, the adventure continues along the scenic **Woodland Trail**:
- **Prehistoric Animal Hunt:** Follow marked woodland trails among mature trees and hunt for life-sized models of Ice Age creatures, including sabre-toothed cats and mammoths.
- **Children's Nature Booklet:** Young explorers can collect a free activity booklet with their cave ticket to solve nature puzzles and learn about Stone Age woodland survival.
- **Seasonal Flora & Fauna:** A relaxing, dappled woodland walk filled with native birds and seasonal wildflowers.

## Stunning Coastal Walks from the Cavern Doorstep

Kents Cavern is situated just a stone's throw from the **South West Coast Path** (and serves as an official stamping point for the South West Coast Path Passport scheme). 

Combine your subterranean tour with some of the finest coastal walking in Devon:
- **Thatcher Point to Anstey’s Cove:** Voted one of the top 50 walks in the UK! Starting right from Kents Cavern, wander down through Ilsham Valley to Thatcher Point for panoramic views of Thatcher Rock island, before looping around the rugged limestone cliffs overlooking the turquoise waters of Anstey's Cove and Redgate Beach.
- **Meadfoot Beach:** Follow the tranquil Ilsham Marine Drive down to the crescent shingle cove of Meadfoot Beach, a favourite relaxation spot for Agatha Christie.

## Food, Amenities & Firestone Kitchen

- **Firestone Kitchen & Café:** Relax before or after your tour at the on-site, dog-friendly café serving delicious breakfasts, brunches, light lunches, artisan coffees, and traditional Devonshire clotted cream teas.
- **Cave-Aged Cheddar:** Sample and take home unique local cheddar aged to perfection inside the humid microclimate of the caves.
- **Gift & Rock Shop:** Browse a treasure trove of genuine fossils, mineral gemstones, jewellery, and prehistoric souvenirs.
- **Dog-Friendly Outdoors:** Dogs on leads are welcome on the sun terrace, the Woodland Trail, and in Firestone Kitchen (only registered assistance dogs are permitted inside the cave chambers).

## Visiting from Tusker Lodge

Kents Cavern is located in the lovely Wellswood neighbourhood of Torquay, just minutes from **[Tusker Lodge](/)**:

- **By Car (5 minutes):** Head east along Babbacombe Road / Ilsham Road (**Sat Nav: TQ1 2JF**). Plentiful on-site parking is available for visitors.
- **On Foot (20–25 minutes):** A pleasant walk through the leafy Wellswood conservation area.
- **By Bus (8–10 minutes):** Catch the frequent **Stagecoach Route 22** or local bus from Torquay centre directly to the Wellswood / Kents Cavern stop.
