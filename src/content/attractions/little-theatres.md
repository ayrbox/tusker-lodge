Nestled in the leafy Meadfoot district of Torquay on St Mark's Road (**TQ1 2EL**), **[TOADS Little Theatre](http://toadslittletheatre.co.uk/)** is one of Torbay's most characterful cultural gems—offering high-quality live drama inside a beautifully converted Victorian church.

---

## A Brief History of TOADS & St Mark's Church

The **TOADS Theatre Company** (originally the Torbay Operatic and Amateur Dramatic Society) was founded in **1944** during the closing years of World War II. For four decades, the company staged popular productions across local Torbay venues before purchasing the redundant Grade II\* listed **St Mark's Church**—designed by Victorian architect Anthony Salvin and originally opened in **1857**.

After a major community fundraising drive (including a 17-week sell-out comedy run in 1985), the building was thoughtfully transformed while preserving its soaring stone arches and rare Victorian stained glass. **The Little Theatre** opened in **1988**, and today even the old choir stalls live on as part of its welcoming theatre bar!

---

## Shows & Activities to Expect

- **Plays, Thrillers & Murder Mysteries:** Gripping dramas, classic whodunnits, and thought-provoking contemporary plays staged throughout the year.
- **Comedies & Farces:** Light-hearted British comedies and audience favourites performed in an intimate 240-seat setting where every seat feels close to the action.
- **Musicals & Special Concerts:** Occasional musical revues, visiting productions, and social interval drinks in the licensed bar.
