Tucked away just a short stroll from Brixham’s bustling harbour, the **[Brixham Heritage Museum](https://www.brixhammuseum.uk/)** is an atmospheric treasure trove celebrating the rich seafaring heritage, characters, and social history of South Devon’s most famous fishing port.

Founded in 1958 and proudly run by passionate local volunteers, this charming community museum is housed in a characterful **1902 former police station**. Behind its red-brick facade lies a collection of over 10,000 objects—bringing centuries of coastal life vividly into focus.

---

## What to Discover Inside

Despite its intimate size, the museum packs an extraordinary range of stories into its galleries:

- **The Mother of Deep-Sea Trawling:** Explore Brixham's rise to international fame in the 19th century as the birthplace of modern deep-sea trawling. Inspect intricately detailed ship models, authentic fishing equipment, navigational instruments, and fascinating displays on historic shipwrecks.
- **The Original 1902 Police Cells:** Step inside the ground floor's authentic Edwardian lock-up cells where local ruffians and smugglers were once held, while the town sergeant and his family lived in the flat upstairs!
- **Victorian Parlour & Nursery:** Walk into beautifully reconstructed period rooms that offer an evocative glimpse of domestic life, fashion, and home comforts in Victorian Devon.
- **Ice Age Discoveries & Coastal Forts:** Uncover Brixham’s prehistoric bone caverns (including Windmill Hill), ancient Neolithic flint tools, and the town’s strategic coastal defences during the Napoleonic Wars and World War II.

## Activities, Events & Family Fun

Brixham Heritage Museum offers plenty to keep visitors of all ages entertained:

- **Family Activities & Pirate Fun:** Young visitors can enjoy activity trails, quizzes, and special craft workshops—including pirate-themed crafting sessions linked with the town's famous maritime festivals.
- **Genealogy & Ancestry Research:** Have family roots in Devon? The museum’s dedicated volunteer archivists offer an acclaimed [family history research service](https://www.brixhammuseum.uk/brixham-heritage-museum/family-history-research/) to help trace fishermen ancestors, old crew records, and local property histories.
- **Dog-Friendly:** Well-behaved four-legged friends on leads are warmly welcomed to accompany you through the museum galleries.

## Visiting Times & Planning Your Visit

- **Admission:** **FREE entry** for all visitors (donations are warmly welcomed to help support the upkeep of this registered charity).
- **Opening Times:** 
  - **Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Saturday:** 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM
  - **Sunday, Monday, and Friday:** Closed
- **Location:** The Old Police Station, New Road, Brixham, Devon, TQ5 8LZ.
- **Parking:** The nearest short-stay parking is at **Brixham Central Car Park** (up to 3 hours). For longer stays, park at **Oxen Cove** or **Freshwater Quarry** and enjoy a picturesque walk along the harbour into town.

## Visiting from Tusker Lodge

Located in Torquay, **[Tusker Lodge](/)** is a wonderful base for visiting Brixham across the bay:

- **By Car:** Approx. **8.5 miles (20–25 minutes)** via the A379 / A3022 around the coastline.
- **By Bus or Ferry:** Take the frequent **Stagecoach Route 12** bus from Torquay harbour directly to Brixham town centre, or take the seasonal **Western Lady Ferry** across Tor Bay for a memorable trip by sea.
- **The Ideal Half-Day Trip:** Combine your museum visit with a stroll along Brixham's historic fish market, an ice cream by the replica Golden Hind, and fresh catch-of-the-day fish and chips on the quay!
