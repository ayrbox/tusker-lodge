# Parking at Tusker Lodge

## On-Site Parking & Local Options

We offer limited private parking for our guests, as well as convenient public parking
options nearby. Please read the details below before your arrival to help plan your
stay smoothly.

## On-Site Guest Parking (Advance Reservation Required)

- **Limited Availability**: Tusker Lodge has 3 on-site parking spaces available.
- **Reservation Essential**: Because spaces are very limited, parking must be reserved in advance. Please request a space directly when making your room booking.
- **Rear Access**: A walkway leads directly from the parking area to the rear entrance of the guest house.
- **Luggage Advisory**: Please note that bringing heavy or bulky luggage along this pathway may be challenging due to steps and terrain.

## Accessibility & Guests with Walking Difficulties

> Important Note: If you or anyone in your party has mobility limitations or walking
> difficulties, please call reception before or while booking. Our team will gladly
> explain the access route, ground layout, and exterior steps so you can decide on
> the most comfortable arrival arrangement.

## Nearby Public Parking (Torquay Harbour Car Park)

If our 3 private spaces are fully booked during your dates, public parking is available close by:

- **Location**: Torquay Harbour Car Park (within easy reach of the lodge).
- **Payment**: This is a council-operated pay-and-display car park. Charges and parking
  fees are the responsibility of the vehicle owner and are payable directly at the car
  park machines or via the local parking app.
