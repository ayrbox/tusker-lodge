## About Us

Nestled in the vibrant heart of Torquay, Tusker Lodge offers a warm
greeting to guests seeking comfort, character, and convenience. Our charming
Victorian guesthouse, a landmark on Falkland Road, combines timeless
architecture with modern amenities, creating a perfect haven for holiday
makers.

Tusker Lodge is a pet-friendly bed and breakfast (B&B) located in the
beautiful coastal town of Torquay, Devon. Our Victorian property offers
comfortable accommodation with modern amenities, perfect for couples,
individuals, and families traveling with pets.

## Chef Sandy

Tusker Lodge is run by Sandy, a chef with many years of experience in the hospitality industry. Sandy is passionate about providing guests with a delicious and enjoyable breakfast experience. She is also a keen baker and enjoys experimenting with new recipes. Sandy is a friendly and welcoming host who is always happy to chat with guests and offer advice on things to see and do in the area.
